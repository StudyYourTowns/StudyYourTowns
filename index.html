Frequently Asked Questions

Before deciding whether these communities should consolidate, residents can request a formal study of what consolidation would actually mean.

How the Local Option Study Process Works

New Jersey's Local Option process allows residents to petition the Local Finance Board directly to establish a Municipal Consolidation Study Commission. The petition does not itself consolidate municipalities.

What does signing the petition actually do?

Signing the petition supports conducting a formal study of municipal consolidation. It does not determine whether consolidation will occur. Any proposal resulting from the study would require a separate approval process.

Could one municipal government cost less than three? How would taxes change in each town? What would happen to state aid, existing debt, public safety and services? Would services improve, decline or remain the same? These are questions for an independent study.
